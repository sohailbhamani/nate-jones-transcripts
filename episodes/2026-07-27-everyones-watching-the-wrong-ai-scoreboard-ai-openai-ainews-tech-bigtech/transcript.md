# Everyone's watching the wrong AI scoreboard #AI #OpenAI #AInews #tech #bigtech

If you're still watching the old AI scoreboard, the one about who shipped the best model in the past month, you're going to miss this story. Here's the shift that is happening across the industry right now. So, first the old scoreboard. For about 2 years, the AI industry ran on a single question, who has the best model? Every earnings call, every launch, every leak got scored against that. And the logic went like this, models are the product, so whoever owns the best model wins, so spend whatever it takes to own the best model. And that logic justified the largest capital buildout in tech history. The big five hyperscalers are on track to spend north of $600 billion on capex this year, up roughly a third from last year, and most of it is AI infra. And to be clear, the model race worked. It produced real capability jumps, clear leaders. We just spent the spring and early summer watching frontier models get so good at cybersecurity work that the federal government has started staggering releases, right? But here's the thing about a race. When the leaders start visibly changing what they're competing on, the race is starting to change.
