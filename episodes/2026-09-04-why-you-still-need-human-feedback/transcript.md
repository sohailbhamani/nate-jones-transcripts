# Why you still need human feedback

Human feedback is so important. I know I've talked about my friends, right? But I really want to call out, if you don't have a community of trusted friends and colleagues who are looking at some part of what you're building, who are saying and thinking and testing and saying, "This is good. This is not good." You're missing out. This is part of what is powerful about the Slack community that I've put together is that people expose their thinking to one another and people critique one another. It's also part of what makes the YouTube comments under these videos special. People expose their thinking. People ask for feedback. I love that. It's so important to find people you can build with because in a sense, a single human reaction that is out of the ordinary can help my brain see something from a new perspective that all of the models missed because the person knows me or the person knows the audience or the person knows the work or the situation differently. And so, I love and I desperately need the feedback from the humans in my life.
