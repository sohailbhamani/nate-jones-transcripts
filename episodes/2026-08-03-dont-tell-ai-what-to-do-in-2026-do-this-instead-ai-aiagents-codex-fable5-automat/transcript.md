  For a couple of years the whole skill was writing a better prompt. You brought the problem, the AI brought the words. That is already outdated. The 2026 version is to hand the AI the problem-finding itself.
  
  Here is what that looks like in practice. Instead of telling it what to fix, I turned it loose. Look at my files, read my Slacks, study how my business actually runs, and then come back and tell me what is worth automating and how. No prompt to perfect, no task to hand it. It has to notice the problem before it solves it. That is a different and far more useful thing than a model that just does what it is told, because most of us are bad at seeing our own bottlenecks in the first place. The interesting part is that when you do this, different models pick different problems, which tells you something about both the models and your business.

# Don't tell AI what to do in 2026. Do this instead #AI #aiagents #Codex #Fable5 #automation

Part of the challenge in 2026 is to ask your AI to pick the problem. You don't just ask it to pick the prompt, you don't just ask it to pick the tool, you ask it to pick the problem. And so, what I did is I said Codex, Fable, Freehand. You can look at all of my local files, you can look at all of my slacks, anything you want to look at that describes my business process. And when you do that, you are obligated to come back to me with a problem definition. And you're obligated to come back to me with a solution and automation.
