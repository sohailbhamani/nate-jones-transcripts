# Why Your Company Doesn't Need the Most Expensive AI Model

Imagine your company uses five times as many AI tokens next month, but spends exactly the same amount. You found way more work for AI. You love AI. You're moving more of it to cheaper models. You're getting more value. The model maker isn't getting more revenue out of you. That is a version of the AI revolution we need to start taking seriously. And yet the model can be so impressive and not be improving a particular decision enough to justify that bill.
