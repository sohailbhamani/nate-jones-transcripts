# Fable 5 is here—but who is it for? #ai #anthropic #shorts

Fable 5 is one of those model launches where I think the immediate question is not is it smarter? Of course it's smarter. It's the new super model. It's the public version of this Mythos class thing Anthropic has been treating like radioactive magic under glass way. The question is, do you have a task big enough for it? And I mean that very practically. Do you have anything in your work where you could say to an AI, go away for 6 hours, 12 hours, 2 days and come back with something I can use? Because I think a lot of us don't. We use AI like a super smart auto complete most of the time, right? Clean up this paragraph, write this report from these 10 docs. Those are the same class of things to Fable. Because a model like Fable comes out and we go, amazing, what do I do with it? It's bigger than all of that. That is our new skill gap. It's not prompt writing, it's task imagination. Can you even imagine a piece of work that is big enough and specific enough and valuable enough to hand to one of these models? I want to make a list of those. So, put one in the comments. What's a task in your domain that would be painful for a human, but where a first class AI artifact would actually be valuable? Not a cute prompt, a real task. I'm going to pick some of those and I'm going to break down how I would give them to Fable.
