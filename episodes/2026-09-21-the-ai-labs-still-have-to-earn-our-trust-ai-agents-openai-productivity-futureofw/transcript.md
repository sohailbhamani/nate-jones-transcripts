# The AI labs still have to earn our trust ... #AI #agents #OpenAI #productivity #futureofwork

You're calling a business and the phone app can bring up something relevant like a confirmation code or a reservation number on the call. Now suppose an agent could work through that whole trip for you. Let's suppose an agent could compare the options against your busy schedule, check the cancellation rules, notice the conflict you missed, come back with a plan ready for approval that accommodates all of that. That's a much larger amount of work. It's an EA sized amount of work. If it did happen reliably, you might pay for it. At the very least, you would feel more comfortable trusting it. Those are the kinds of outcomes the labs need to deliver reliably to justify a much bigger recurring relationship with you. And by reliably, I mean nearly 100% of the time. That's the bar.
