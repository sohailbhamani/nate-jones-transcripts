# What is Omarchy?  #OS #AI #agents

Every operating system you have ever used is a pile of decisions someone else made years before you sat down to it. Where do the notification goes? What opens on startup? What are you allowed to change? All of those decisions are decisions you have been living with since long before the age of AI. That is changing. Omacia is a Linux desktop from DHH and it's built so you can point an AI agent your own OS and go back in time and change those decisions. That's the future. Customizing your existing OS so it works for you.
