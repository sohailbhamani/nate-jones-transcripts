# Big firms are safe  Startups are weak  The middle is doomed

The giants are not nearly as vulnerable as the story might suggest. Many of them have motes that AI cannot effectively erode. And the startups may not be quite as threatening as advertised because the capabilities that they are selling are often getting cheaper by the month, which is a problem if your whole value proposition is the capability and not the distribution. Ironically, the place where firms are actually in trouble is in between because the conventional wisdom is looking to just at the small firms and the big firms and missing the entire middle tier of the professional services and software industries. Think the marketing agency with 40 employees, the IT consultancy that's been around for 15 years, the software development shop that serves mid-market clients, the design firm that built a reputation on being reliable and professional over the last couple of decades. Those firms are getting squeezed from both directions. From below by tiny teams that are using AI to produce work that's nearly indistinguishable from what a mid-tier firm can get done. And from above by giants with distribution advantages that the mid-tier firm cannot match. They have no obvious escape route.
