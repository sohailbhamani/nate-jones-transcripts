# AI Is Cheaper to Copy Than Create #Shorts #AI

The incentive to distill frontier models would exist even if China and the United States were close allies. It would exist even if there were no military applications at all. It exists because of the most basic force in information economics. The cost of generating intelligence is astronomically higher than the cost of copying that intelligence. Which brings us to what distillation actually produces. Because this is the part that matters for anyone who uses AI models to do real work. Hopefully many of you and me. And it's the part that the discourse is completely ignoring. So here's what copying intelligence does to intelligence. Distillation does not produce a copy of the original model. It produces a compression. And that compression, like a lossy MP3, has characteristics that matter enormously for anyone building real systems on top of these models.
