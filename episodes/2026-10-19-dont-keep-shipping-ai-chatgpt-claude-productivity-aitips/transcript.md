  When you use AI now, you pick a model and an effort level, and they can feel like the same choice. They’re not. The model is about how hard the problem is. A basic deck, spreadsheet, or email works fine on a lighter model, while a hard problem you want to think through and argue about needs a stronger one. Effort is how hard that model thinks, like giving the car more gas instead of switching cars. More gas lets it go further and plan longer tasks, but sometimes it over-solves and buries you in detail you don’t need, so I often dial it back.
