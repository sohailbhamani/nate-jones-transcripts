Just give me the answer. I just want the answer. The answer for you is to pick the model that makes you feel most comfortable doing your hardest work. Because if you're if you're pushing on the model, if you're doing your hardest work, and the model helps you get that done, that's the model you're going to want to lean on. So, when in doubt, go with the model that picks your hardest work.
