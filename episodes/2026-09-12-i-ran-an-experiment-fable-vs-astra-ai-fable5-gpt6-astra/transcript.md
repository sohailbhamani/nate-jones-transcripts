# I ran an experiment: Fable vs Astra #AI #Fable5 #GPT6 #Astra

I gave one prompt to two models, and I got two completely different apps, both trying to solve the exact same problem, copy-paste help. Fable built Ledge, a narrow list that slides in from the right side of my screen. Astra decided on Shelf, a wide bar across the bottom with nice big cards and a preview pane. They both got the exact same brief. They both got the same amount of time, and both of them are functional apps. They're good apps, and I'm a little bit surprised myself.
