# The AI skill nobody talks about (and it isn't prompting) #AI #prompting #productivity #tech

So, the 2025 person types a request and they're asking for a PowerPoint deck, right? They get back something that's about 80% correct. Person B sits down with 2026 prompting skills. They write a structured specification in 11 minutes. They take longer to prompt. Then they hand it off to the same chatbot, but they're thinking of it and using it as an autonomous agent. In other words, they are now doing a week's worth of work in a morning, easily. Same model, same Tuesday, 10x scale.
