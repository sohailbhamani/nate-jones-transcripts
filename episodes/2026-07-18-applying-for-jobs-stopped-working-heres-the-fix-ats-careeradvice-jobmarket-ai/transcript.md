The success rate on applications has dropped to something like half a percent. Not 5% like 0.4%. This is an arms race where both sides continue to escalate and everybody loses. Candidates invest more in gaming filters, companies invest more in building filters. The filters just keep getting smarter, the gaming gets smarter. 80 88% 88% of employers admit their own systems cause them to miss qualified people. Everyone knows the infrastructure is failing. Everyone keeps trying to win anyway using the same system.
