  When someone asks me which model to use, they want me to name one. I never do, because the question starts in the wrong place. Do not look at the model first. Look at your best work, and look at how you actually got there.
  
  Notice the process. Where does your thinking happen? Is it a long messy brain-dump you then shape, or a tight back-and-forth, or something you sketch and refine? Most of your best work does not even start with the model, it starts with how you think. Once you can see that loop clearly, the question finally makes sense: which model helps me move through that loop faster and get to my best self? For one person that is a model that reads a huge rambling prompt and just runs. For another it is a model that argues back. Same question, different answer, and the only way to get yours right is to start from your own process instead of a leaderboard.

# This is the best way to choose an AI model

My best tip when people say, "What model do I pick?" is to not look at the model first. Instead, look at your best work and look at how you get there. And it maybe not with the model. Look at the process you use for thinking. And then start to ask yourself which model helps me to accelerate that loop that gets me to my best
