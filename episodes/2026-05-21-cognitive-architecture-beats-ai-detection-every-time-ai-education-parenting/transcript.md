# Cognitive Architecture Beats AI Detection Every Time #ai #education #parenting

Karpathy also said something that I think every parent and teacher needs to hear right now. Quote, you will never be able to detect the use of AI in homework, full stop. He's right. The arms race between AI writing detection and AI writing generation was over before it started and the people who are selling AI detection to schools are wrecking the outcome of kids. They are judging kids based on a heuristic that is mathematically impossible to implement in a correct way. You cannot detect AI in homework, full stop. The educational response cannot be better detection and I see too many cases where students are being pushed out of school for something they did not do because of a tool that administrators blindly believe is accurate just because of the messaging on the tin. You can't detect AI writing, full stop. It has to be a fundamental rethinking of what we're measuring and why, which is long overdue anyway.
