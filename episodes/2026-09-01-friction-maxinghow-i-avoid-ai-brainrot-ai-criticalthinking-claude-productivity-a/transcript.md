# Friction maxing...How I avoid AI brainrot #AI #criticalthinking #Claude #productivity #aitools

Friction maxing, that's what I do all day with AI and it's why I don't think it's rotting my brain. Most AI use is friction removal. I totally get it and I talk about it a lot on this channel. You ask, you get an answer and you move on, right? It's fast, it's clean, it's done. But I run it back the other way, too, and that's what this video is about. I make the AI work harder on purpose. But every day, I move between Codex and Grok and Claude and about 10 people that I trust, not because I want all of those opinions for fun, but because I want to find the thing that breaks my assumptions, the thing that breaks the answer that everybody else is agreeing on. From the outside, this just looks like an elaborate system of passing the work around, but what I'm hunting for is disagreement. Every disagreement is like a rep for my brain and the work comes out better because whatever survives four, five, six, 10 rounds of argument is never what the model first handed to me, is it?
