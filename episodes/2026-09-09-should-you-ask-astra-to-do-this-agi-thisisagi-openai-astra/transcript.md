# Should you ask Astra to do this? #AGI #thisisAGI #openai #astra

I'm not saying that you can or should turn on Astra, say, "Here's my credit card." and just let it move the family while you go to sleep. No one is trying to say that. I am not saying that here. But, I am saying that you can throw that scale of work at Astra. You can actually throw it a move if you're moving, and it's going to make a huge dent in the massive amount of life administration work that goes with a decision like that.
