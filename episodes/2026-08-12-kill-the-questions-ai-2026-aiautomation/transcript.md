  Every support team has been optimizing the same thing for years: speed. How fast can we answer the ticket, reply to the email, close the loop. AI made that part almost free, so now everyone can answer fast, and answering fast has quietly stopped being the thing that matters.
  
  Look at what actually happens before a single reply goes out. You find the email, you check the payment, you dig through Slack, you send another invite, you write another apology, you close another ticket. That is the hidden work, and most of it exists because the customer had to ask in the first place. So the better question is not how do we answer people faster. It is how do we make sure they never had to ask at all. When you point AI at the whole process instead of just the reply, the win is not a faster inbox. It is a smaller one.

In 2026, you're looking at the whole process. You're looking at all of the hidden work that you do to get there. We would find the email, we would check the payment, we would look in Slack, we would send another invite, we would write another apology, we would close another ticket. And that changes the question, because the question is not just how do we answer these people fast? That's 2024. It's how do we make sure people don't have to ask us for this?
