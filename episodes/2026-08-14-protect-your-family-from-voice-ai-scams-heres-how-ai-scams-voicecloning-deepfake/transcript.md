# Protect your family from voice AI scams. Here's how  #AI #scams #voicecloning #deepfakes

So, you should have a password with your family. Something that you would never normally use in conversation that the whole family knows that if you use that word, it's really you. But, if someone clones your voice, if someone clones your likeness, and they call, that that digital persona is not going to know that secret word or that secret phrase, and they're not going to be able to guess. And that way your family will know if there's some sort of digital demand for ransom, it's not really you.
