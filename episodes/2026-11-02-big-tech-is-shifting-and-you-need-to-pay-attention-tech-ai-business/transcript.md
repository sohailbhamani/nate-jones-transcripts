# Big Tech is shifting, and you need to pay attention #tech #ai #business

On October 27th, Salesforce made Microsoft's agents much, much easier to use. And on the same day, Oracle said it's building a way to bring its own apps, its Oracle apps, into Chat GPT. Rivals are opening doors for rivals. Nobody does that out of generosity. Every one of these companies is chasing the same seat. They're chasing the software you depend on to get work done across everything else you own. If you're running a team, you're picking who gets that seat right now.
