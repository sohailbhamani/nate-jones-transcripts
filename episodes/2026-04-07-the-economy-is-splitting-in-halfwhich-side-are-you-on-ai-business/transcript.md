# The economy is splitting in half—which side are you on? #ai #business

If your firm mostly sells first-layer work, the drafting, the analysis, the code, you got a big The work is becoming commoditized, your competitors can can produce it for similar quality, often at a lower cost. The thing you are selling is no longer special. It's no longer scarce. If your firm's value was always in the second layer, though, you're in a much better spot. It was if it was always in the relationships, if it was always in the accountability, in the good taste. You still need to produce good first-layer work, but now you can produce it cheaper, and you still depend for value on that second-layer good judgment and taste to compete. And of course, if you're in the third layer, you're in a really good spot because the fundamental economics favor you at this point.
