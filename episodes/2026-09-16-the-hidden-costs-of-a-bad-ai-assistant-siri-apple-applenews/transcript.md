  Here is a cost of bad AI that never shows up in a benchmark or a keynote. When an assistant is slow and a little dumb for long enough, people quietly stop asking it for things. That is the real damage.

That is the hole Apple is in. # The hidden costs of a bad AI assistant #siri #apple #applenews

Apple has to deal with Siri, because Siri has been annoyingly slow and annoyingly dumb for a long, long time. I am tired of asking for something, watching the screen glow, and waiting, and then sometimes the result gives me a whole new reason to wonder why I bothered. It's just a disaster. There is a cost to that which doesn't appear in any kind of model or brand benchmark. People learn to stop asking. If doing it yourself is quicker than explaining to the stupid AI assistant and checking whether it understood, you're going to do it yourself and be annoyed with the brand. After enough of those experiences, Apple can announce all the intelligence it wants, you're still going to reach for the app, you're still going to search your email, you're still going to open the AI you do trust. I don't want to stand here negotiating with my phone over something I could have had done already. I don't have the time for that. Apple is going to have to earn its way back into the AI assistant moment.
