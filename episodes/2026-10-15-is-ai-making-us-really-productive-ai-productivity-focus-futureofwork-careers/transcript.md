# Is AI making us really productive? #AI #productivity #focus #futureofwork #careers

AI can also make distraction look extremely productive. I can tell you that. By 5:00 you might have lots of prototypes, you might have three plans, you have a very elaborate testing version of an idea that you weren't pursuing when you woke up. You did things, right? But how much of that work got far enough to make a difference to anybody? If you keep adding projects because each one looks easier and easier and easier, it's easy to consume all the extra capacity that AI gives you and still leave the important work unfinished.
