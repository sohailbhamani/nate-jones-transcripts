# Are Chinese AI models actually catching up?

The true frontier in American labs is what is not released. It is what is in the lab. So when you think about the narrative that Chinese models are catching up, you have got to start benchmarking correctly. They are still about six or seven months behind just like they were a year ago. And I see a narrative where it's like, wow, they're almost close to fable. They're almost close to, you know, 5.6. They'll pass them by the end of the year. I've seen that take. That's just incorrect. The model makers are well past that now. It'll be another four, five, 6 months before we see what they've got internally. They will do even more safety testing because it's a risk now. But they're not catching up. And there's no sign that they're imminently catching up. The closed source models in anthropic and open AI have a tremendous lead and they're not letting up right
