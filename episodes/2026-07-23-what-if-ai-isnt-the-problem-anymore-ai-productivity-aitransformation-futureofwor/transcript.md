  Most people bought AI and then pointed it at the exact same to-do list they had two years ago. The emails still get written, just faster. The decks still get built, just cheaper. It feels like progress, and the tools genuinely are better, so it is easy to call that a transformation.

Has your task list changed in the last 12 months? In the last six? In the last three? Has what you have asked AI to do shifted? Or are you doing your old list faster and cheaper and calling that AI transformation? Because if it's the old list, nothing is wrong with your tools. You have an imagination shortage.
