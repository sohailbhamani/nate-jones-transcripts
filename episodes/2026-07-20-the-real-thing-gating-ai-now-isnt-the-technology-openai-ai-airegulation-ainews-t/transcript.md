# The real thing gating AI now isn't the technology #OpenAI #AI #AIregulation #AInews #tech

The frontier labs have hit a point where the binding constraint on their business is not capability, and actually it's not even compute, it's permission. Washington can now delay a flagship release with a phone call. And in that world, political alignment is not corporate social responsibility, right? It's just infrastructure to get your model out. OpenAI is trying to buy regulatory headroom the way Meta is monetizing data centers.
