# Fix your operating model or lose at AI #ai #strategy

If you can't go fast with agents, it is on you as a leader. It is not on you to blame your employees for using tokens. It is on you to understand how you framed the challenge for them, how you are framing the opportunity in change management, how you are enabling the business to stand up agentic pipelines that are truly end-to-end and don't presume a bunch of human handoffs in the middle. And if you fix this piecemeal, if you say, "We're going to do our PRD piece, we're going to do our PR handoff piece and PR review piece, you run a risk of having a bunch of problems that are downstream of a piecemeal approach to problem-solving. Basically, if you fix agents piecemeal, you end up playing a game of band-aids. You're always fixing the next downstream bottleneck. You're not thinking about how to do this end-to-end, and you end up with a bunch of unpredictable piles of generated work that clog up human review gates at unpredictable points in your organization. You want to be at a point can help the system to learn and help your humans to help the system to learn.
