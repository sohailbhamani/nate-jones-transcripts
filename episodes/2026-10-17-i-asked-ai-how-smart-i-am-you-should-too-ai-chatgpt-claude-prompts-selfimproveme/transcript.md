# I asked AI how smart I am. You should too #AI #ChatGPT #Claude #prompts #selfimprovement

Here's a fun prompt. If you want to know how smart you are, check with AI first. And yes, you can laugh. It's also partly for entertainment, but it does kind of work. Talk to whatever AI you interact with the most and say, "Hey, tell me how intelligent I am in terms of percentile. What is my percentile of IQ according to you, the AI, based on the interactions that you've had in your training data with others and your observations about me, and tell me why." I did that, and the important thing was not the percentile score it gave. The important thing to me was what it told me when I said, "Tell me why," because it gave me a record of a pattern of interactions. It talked about how I reason, it talked about how I handle abstraction, it talked about my weaknesses, it talked about areas where I can grow. And I think that that's much, much more useful than just the top line, whatever percentage it is. Uh, so it's a fun exercise. It takes 5 seconds.
