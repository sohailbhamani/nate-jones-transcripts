# The bridge between hand-waving and doing it all #ai #innovation #fable5

I think Fable 5 is exposing a weird gap in how we work. For most of history, we've had two modes, right? One mode is handwaving. You tell someone, you go figure this out. Please make this better. You look into this and you hope they understand what you meant. And maybe you have a conversation. The other mode is doing everything yourself. You get super detailed. You make the spreadsheet. You write the memo. You check every single line. You basically become the machine. AI creates this third mode that we're not very good at yet. It's this middle layer where you say, "Here's the outcome. Here's the bar. Here's what good looks like. Here are the constraints. Here's what I don't want. Now go produce the artifact." And that is not how most people have been trained to look. And with cheap models, you can kind of get away with being sloppy there because they don't run very well. But with a model like Fable 5, especially if it can work for hours, the sloppiness gets real expensive real fast. The model may be capable of magic, but you still have to sponsor the magic correctly. You have to know what spell you're asking for. That's the phrase I keep coming back to. Practical magic. Not vibecoded magic. Not make this better. Practical magic. The ability to describe a hard valuable task clearly enough that a frontier model can go get it. That is going to be a real skill.
