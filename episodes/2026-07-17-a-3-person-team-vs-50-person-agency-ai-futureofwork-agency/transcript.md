# A 3-person team vs 50-person agency #AI #FutureOfWork #agency

A team of three people with good AI tools can now produce as much work as that 50-person agency produces. Now, is it identical? No. But, is it close enough that clients start to wonder why they're paying for 50 people? Yes. The tiny team is faster, it's cheaper, and it's increasingly competitive on quality. They might not have the agency's reputation, but they have a portfolio, and they can price aggressively.
