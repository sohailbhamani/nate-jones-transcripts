# You're learning AI wrong. Here's the fix #AI #Management #Leadership #FutureOfWork

The survivors figured out one thing. AI isn't a tool skill, it's a management skill. That is not a Copilot specific lesson, that's a larger take. And that changes everything about how you and I need to be training. I think Ethan Mollick puts this really well when he says the best users of AI are good managers. They're good teachers. The skills that make you good at AI are not prompting skills, they're people skills.
