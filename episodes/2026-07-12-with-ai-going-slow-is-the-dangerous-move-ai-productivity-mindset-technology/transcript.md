# With AI, going slow is the dangerous move #AI #productivity #mindset #technology

I compare AI to riding a bike. If you are going slow on a bike, it's really hard to balance and you feel like you're never going to catch up. But experientially, when you go faster on a bike, the steadiness increases. The way to balance gets easier and kids have so much trouble learning this. They think if they go slower they'll be safer, but they're actually safer going faster. And that is what you have to learn with AI. You're actually safer leaning in and going faster than you are going slower.
