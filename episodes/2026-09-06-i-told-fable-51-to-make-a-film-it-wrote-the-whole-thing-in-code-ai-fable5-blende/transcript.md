# I told Fable 5.1 to make a film. It wrote the whole thing in code. #AI #Fable5 #Blender #3D #design

The full film runs for 37 seconds and Fable built the house, the terrain, the interiors, the lighting, the landscaping, the whole camera path in code. It rendered the stills, it inspected them, it changed the scenes, it checked a motion preview, and then it rendered the final sequence. Trees are a bit stylized, some of the glasses a bit simple. There are things you could improve. If you're an editor, don't worry, you still have your day job. But if you are trying to get a concept across, if you're trying to communicate to a client and you have something visual to say, it's a big deal to say, "Hey, Fable, make this into a video in Blender." And come back in an hour and you get a concept, right? It doesn't have to be architectural. It can be literally anything that takes video to communicate because Fable's perfectly able to take an idea you give it, turn it into code, make a 3D model, pan a camera around it digitally, and create a video.
